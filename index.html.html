Some people arrive in your life the way seasons do — quietly, until one day you realize you can't quite remember what things felt like before them. I've never been good at saying things plainly, so instead of a message, I built something a little longer, a little slower — a way of saying the one thing I keep almost saying out loud.


Call it a birthday wish, if you want a simple word for it. I'd call it the closest I've come to being honest. Read on — there's more here than you'd expect.

one more thing

Before You Go

You usually reply through a story only I can see. I don't mind that — but if you ever wanted to just message me directly, the door's open.

Thank you.

If this were an ordinary letter, I'd write it the way people usually do — "hope your year is wonderful," "you deserve the world," all the things that are true but say nothing. I wanted to write something that actually meant what it said.

I think there's a particular kind of honesty that only shows up in the things we build instead of the things we say — a page like this one says more than I probably would out loud, and maybe that's the point. Not everything true needs to be spoken to be real. Some things are truer for having been shown instead.

I don't know what this year holds for either of us. But I know I wanted to be the kind of friend who notices — the cat photos, the quiet tree, the way you look at things when you think no one's watching. I noticed. I still do.

So happy birthday. Read this however you'd like to read it — as a friend being sincere, or as something a little more than that. I'll let you decide which. I already know which one I meant.

Before You Go

You usually reply through a story only I can see. I don't mind that — but if you ever wanted to just message me directly, the door's open.

Thank you.

If this were an ordinary letter, I'd write it the way people usually do — "hope your year is wonderful," "you deserve the world," all the things that are true but say nothing. I wanted to write something that actually meant what it said.

I think there's a particular kind of honesty that only shows up in the things we build instead of the things we say — a page like this one says more than I probably would out loud, and maybe that's the point. Not everything true needs to be spoken to be real. Some things are truer for having been shown instead.

I don't know what this year holds for either of us. But I know I wanted to be the kind of friend who notices — the cat photos, the quiet tree, the way you look at things when you think no one's watching. I noticed — that's really all this page is. Someone noticing.

So happy birthday. I won't tell you how to read this. I'll just say — I chose every word here on purpose.
